# Deity & Ride Assets Generation Tracker

This tracker documents all deities and rides (vaahanas) in the Avani Kids Learning App, their attributes, and image asset generation status using `nanobanana` (image generation).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

All assets maintain visual harmony with the chibi toddler-sticker aesthetic:
- **Character Style**: Adorable chibi cartoon deities and baby animal rides, cute toddler-friendly characters, large heads with big expressive sparkling eyes, sweet gentle smiles.
- **Outlines & Shading**: Bold clean outlines, smooth vibrant digital vector coloring, rich warm palette.
- **Composition**: Centered figures, white die-cut sticker borders around the outlines, transparent backgrounds.
- **Aspect Ratio**: `3:4` for Deities, `1:1` for Rides.

## 🛡️ Fallback Architecture

1. **Deities**: Handled by `DeityOptionCard.vue` and `DeityCard.vue`:
   - Automatically loads `${id}.png`.
   - On `@error`, falls back seamlessly to `${id}.svg`.
2. **Rides**: Handled by `VaahanaOption.vue`:
   - Automatically loads `option.image` (`${id}.png`).
   - On `@error` or if missing, falls back to the large, colorful, high-contrast Toddler Emoji card (`option.emoji`).

---

## 🛠️ Transparent Background Processing Script

Saved in `scratch/make_transparent.py`:

```bash
python3 scratch/make_transparent.py "<input_image.jpg>" "<output_image.png>" 25
```
